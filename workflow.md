## 2026-11-09 / Step 3: adapting the core–periphery model to a knowledge economy

- Action and reason: I asked whether Krugman's two sectors (agriculture and manufacturing) could be replaced by manufacturing and knowledge work, since agriculture is irrelevant in these cities.
- Change made: Learnt that the model depends on the *roles* of its sectors, not their names: one mobile sector with increasing returns, and one immobile anchor. The version with mobile skilled workers already exists in the literature, the "footloose entrepreneur" model (Forslid & Ottaviano 2003). Housing and congestion costs (Helpman 1998) add a force that pushes activity out of the core. This gives a sharper prediction: graduates and knowledge jobs should concentrate in Milan faster than population and total jobs.
